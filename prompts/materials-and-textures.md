# Materials & Textures Prompts

Use `/pattern` in the Gemini CLI to generate seamless textures for materials.

## Fabrics
```
/pattern Seamless linen fabric texture, natural off-white, subtle woven detail, high resolution
/pattern Seamless velvet upholstery in deep emerald green, soft directional sheen
/pattern Seamless herringbone tweed in charcoal and ivory, close-up weave detail
```

## Wood & Stone
```
/pattern Seamless light oak wood grain, smooth finish, horizontal grain direction
/pattern Seamless Carrara marble, white with subtle gray veining, polished surface
/pattern Seamless concrete texture, fine aggregate, light gray, matte finish
```

## Wallpaper
```
/pattern Seamless Art Deco geometric wallpaper pattern, gold and cream, repeating fan motif
/pattern Seamless botanical wallpaper, large tropical leaves on white background
/pattern Seamless grasscloth wallpaper texture, natural woven sisal, warm beige
```
